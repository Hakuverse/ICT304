## What we agreed

SARAH will help a teacher identify students who may need support and suggest a suitable action. It should work for one student or a class list, rather than only giving a prediction for one person at a time.

We chose the UCI Student Performance Math dataset as our starting point. A final grade below 10 out of 20 is labelled High Risk; a grade of 10 or above is labelled Low Risk.
